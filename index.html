What should I study, and what does “done” mean?

Follow the courses in order. Use the course links to study the source material. Then open the matching course section below to see why you are taking it, what you must understand, what to practise, and what you need to prepare before calling it complete.

1. Course sequence

Take these in this order. Don't run all courses in parallel.

How to use the links: “Course” opens the official course page or documentation. “YouTube” is a direct video-search link for that course/topic, so you can choose the relevant lecture or playlist. Prefer the official course page for assignments and updated materials.

2. Course completion guide

Open one course at a time. Complete its checklist before moving on.

3. Free certificate & badge tracker

Track credentials separately from actual course understanding.

A completion certificate records that you finished a course. A badge records that you passed a module assessment. Neither replaces the projects in this plan. Only claim a credential after the provider confirms completion.
